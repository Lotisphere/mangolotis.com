FEATURES02

  • Up to 4 players (host + 3)
  • Join through Steam friends or the in-game F5 menu
  • Enemies, attacks and health stay in sync — enemies chase and fight whoever they're targeting
  • Your friends appear as knights holding the weapon they actually have equipped
  • Enemy health scales slightly with party size
  • Progress belongs to the host; everyone keeps their own character
  • English, 中文 and 日本語 menus

HOW TO INSTALL03

  1. Download the zip.
  2. Extract it into the game folder (steamapps/common/QueensDomain) so that xinput1_3.dll lands in QueensDomain/Binaries/Win64/.
  3. Launch the game from Steam.
  4. Press F5 in game.

HOW TO PLAY TOGETHER04

  • Host: F5 → Co-op → Create room.
  • Friends: right-click the host in the Steam friends list → Join Game, or F5 → Co-op → Friends' rooms.
